# Qwen3.5-27B on one 8× H100 node: 50-step results (2026-10-17)

One p5.48xlarge (8× H100 80 GB, NVLink), us-east-2a, 02:44-~10:35 UTC (~7.9 h). Plan: `plan_27b_50step.md`. Queues: `queue_27b_all.sh`, `queue_27b_more.sh`; their output in `box_logs_20261017/`.

Equal work in every run: Megatron-Bridge's exported CORD-v2 rows in Bridge's order, 64 rows per step (1 per microbatch per DP copy), every row padded to 2048, token-mean loss over the step, AdamW lr 1e-5 constant, betas 0.9/0.95, eps 1e-8, no weight decay, no clipping, no warm-up, fp32 master weights and fp32 Adam. Gradients accumulated in bf16 on Bridge and rdsp (27B does not fit otherwise), fp32 on TorchTitan (its default). rdsp: fused AdamW, optimizer groups of 250M. Bridge: precision-aware optimizer (fp32 master as remainders). No profiler. Step = median of steps 6-50.

## What the numbers say

1. **Fastest layout: TP2 × PP2 × DP2 with recompute**, ~17.5 s on both Bridge and rdsp, 23-31% faster than the best TP4 run (rdsp TP4 × PP2 without recompute, 22.78 s).
2. **The pipeline is needed at 27B, for memory.** The TP2 layouts only fit with PP: TP2 × DP4, the no-pipeline layout that won at 9B, does not hold 27B.
3. **Where memory allows, less pipeline and more data parallelism is faster** (same TP, same recompute):
   - TP4: DP2 beats PP2 by 24% (Bridge 26.95 vs 35.26) and 17% (rdsp 29.26 vs 35.37).
   - TP2: PP2 × DP2 beats PP4 by 29% (Bridge 17.47 vs 24.70) and 22% (rdsp 17.70 vs 22.65).
4. **TP degree matters more than recompute.** TP2 with recompute beats TP4 without it.
5. **Bridge against rdsp, same layout:** rdsp faster on TP4 × PP2 (10%) and TP2 × PP4 (8%); Bridge faster on TP4 × DP2 (8%) and TP2 × PP2 × DP2 (1.3%); even on TP4 × PP2 with recompute (0.3%).
6. **TorchTitan, as configured** (its default compile: 7 small regions such as the loss, activations, norms and rotary embedding, `models/qwen3_5/model.py:355`, no whole-layer compile; CUDA graphs off; first run of this model): 1.5-2.9× slower. Its FSDP lets TP4 × DP2 fit without recompute (68.5 GiB), and that layout is its fastest.
7. **Vision on its own GPU:** only rdsp can train 27B this way on one node (language over 7 GPUs as TP4 + TP2 + TP1 stages, recompute). MIMO caps the language model at 4 GPUs and runs out of memory.

Open, needing a profile: why PP loses to DP at equal recompute (hypothesis: the slower of the two stages sets the pace; stage 0 holds the vision encoder and embedding, stage 1 the 1.27B output layer and the loss); why recompute costs rdsp more than Bridge on TP4 × PP2 (+55% against +39%); where TorchTitan's time goes; TorchTitan's step-1 gradient norm (101.7 against Megatron's 23.9; step 2 matches: 13.5 against 15.3).
